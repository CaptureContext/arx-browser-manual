Arx keeps a history of the pages you visit in each [profile](/profiles/profiles), on your Mac. The **History** page in the Library lists it by day, newest first.

::preview{scene="history"}
::

## Open it

- :keys{combo="⌘ Y"} (**Show History** in the Archive menu), which also shows the sidebar if it was hidden.
- The **History** section of the Library, or **Show History** from the Library button's right-click menu.

The page shows the history of the profile of the space you're in. Private windows keep no history, so they have no History page.

## Find a page

Days are headed **Today**, **Yesterday**, then by date. Each entry shows the page's title, the time you last visited it and its address. A page you visit again moves to the top with the new time; the history keeps one entry per address.

Type in the search at the top to narrow the list. Every word you type has to appear in the title or the address.

Click an entry to open it in a new tab. The Library stays open, so you can open several. Right-click an entry for **Open in New Tab** too.

## Delete

Right-click an entry, or a day's heading, for:

- **Delete from History**: removes that page at once.
- **Delete This Day…**: removes every page visited that day.
- **Delete All from** the site **…**: removes every page from that site, with or without `www.`.

Deleting a day or a site asks first and says how many pages go. They go even if the search hides some of them. Deleting can't be undone, and it doesn't close open tabs or touch cookies and saved pages.

The command bar's **Delete from History** ([⌘K on a result](/command-bar/command-bar#actions)) does the same, and the History page updates in every window as soon as anything is deleted.

To clear a profile's whole history, or the last hour or day of it, use [Clear browsing data](/profiles/clear-browsing-data).

::note
Pictures that [hover previews](/tabs/sidebar#tabs-that-arent-loaded) saved of a page stay when you delete just that page. Clearing browsing data removes them.
::
