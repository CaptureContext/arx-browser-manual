When no tab is open, the window shows the homepage: a large Arx mark in glass and hologram over slowly drifting dust, and a tip under it. The page is partly see-through, so the space's theme shows behind it. The pointer stays the regular arrow.

::

## Tips

There are about a hundred tips, covering tabs and folders, spaces and profiles, Little Arx and Peek, split view, capture, media, the command bar, links, privacy, extensions, assistants and Settings. The tips name what you can do and the keys for it, such as :keys{combo="⌘ T"} for a new tab and :keys{combo="⌘ S"} to hide the sidebar. They use your own shortcuts from **Settings → Shortcuts**: a tip whose command has no shortcut isn't shown. A new tip comes up every few seconds; the line under it shows how long until the next.

To step through them yourself, click the page, then press :keys{combo="←"} or :keys{combo="→"}. The tip you pick stays for a whole turn before the next comes up.

The homepage moves only while Arx is the active app. With **Reduce motion** on in **System Settings → Accessibility → Display**, it holds still.

## Customize it

Arx is testing a homepage you can change. Turn on **Customizable homepage** in **Settings → Advanced → Feature Flags** and relaunch Arx, or quit Arx and run:

```bash
defaults write dev.capturecontext.arx ARX_CUSTOMIZABLE_HOMEPAGE -string on
```

A small brush button then sits in the homepage's bottom-right corner. Click it to open the controls over the page, and click it again or press :keys{combo="Esc"} to close them. Changes show as you make them and apply to every window.

| Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | **Centered**, **Watermark** (a huge mark in the corner) or **Off**; its size, opacity and blend mode, kept separately for Centered and Watermark; and **Glass** and **Hologram**. |
| **Pointer** | **Regular**, or a **Circle**, **Square** or **Triangle** with its own size, fill and stroke. The shape is the pointer only over the homepage. |
| **Effect** | **Ink trail**: the pointer leaves ink that dries. Choose a solid, shifting or rainbow color, its width, how fast it dries, how soft it is and whether it narrows as it dries. |
| **Guide** | **Shortcuts**, a list of the keys that open a page, or **Hints**, the rolling tips. With the logo as a watermark or off, the guide can sit in the center or the bottom-left corner. |
| **Background** | **Sky** (with stars and clouds), **Sparkles**, **Dots** that move away from the pointer, a **Grid** that ripples where you click, **Warp**, **Film**, **Silk**, **Topography** or **Dust**. |

**Presets** has **CaptureContext**, the homepage Arx starts with, and **Classic**, the small quiet mark from before. **Reset** goes back to CaptureContext.

To go back to the standard homepage, turn the flag off in the same place, or quit Arx and run:

```bash
defaults delete dev.capturecontext.arx ARX_CUSTOMIZABLE_HOMEPAGE
```

Your changes stay saved for when you turn it on again.
