Peek opens a link in a card over the page you were reading. The page behind shrinks back a little and dims, and stays where it was. The card grows from the link you clicked and shrinks back into it when it closes.

While the Peek is open, the tab you opened it from shows the peeked site's icon in the sidebar: at the right end of its row or of its part of a Split View row, or as a small badge on the corner of its Favorite.

## Open a Peek

| How | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| :keys{combo="⇧ Click"} a link | **Shift-click a link to open**: Peek (default), Little Arx, New Tab or New Window |
| :keys{combo="⇧ ⌘ P"} (**Open Focused Link in Peek**) on a link you moved to with :keys{combo="Tab"} | Change the key in **Settings → Shortcuts** |
| :keys{combo="⇧ Return"} on a link you moved to with :keys{combo="Tab"} | Same setting as Shift-click |
| Force Click a link on a trackpad | **Force Click a link to open**: Peek (default), Little Arx, New Tab, or Look Up, which keeps the macOS dictionary |
| Click a link to another site in a Favorite | **Open links to other sites from Favorites**: Peek (default), Little Arx, New Tab or Current Tab |
| Click a link to another site in a pinned tab | **Open links to other sites from pinned tabs**: Peek, Little Arx, New Tab or Current Tab (default) |
| A page in a Favorite or a pinned tab opens a new tab, as Gmail's account switch does | The same two settings |
| **Open in Peek** in a link's context menu on the page | |
| **Peek** in a tab's menu | |

These settings are in **Settings → Links**, and each one chooses where its gesture opens a link, so Peek is only the default. **New Tab** opens the link behind the page, right after its tab, or in front when **When a link opens in a new tab, switch to it** is on in **Settings → General**. For a Shift-click, **New Window** is what other browsers do. A gesture set to Peek on a page where Peek can't open falls back to what the click does without Arx: a new window for a Shift-click, Look Up for a Force Click, and the tab itself for a link from a Favorite or a pinned tab.

Links in private windows, links that aren't web pages (such as `mailto:`), and links on a page that's already in a Peek don't open in Peek, so the menu leaves the item out there.

**Open Focused Link in Peek** is also in the command bar and the **Tab** menu. It opens the link that has keyboard focus, in the page or in a frame. With no click to grow from, the card opens from the page itself. It does nothing when a text field, a button or anything else that isn't a link has focus, in a private window, or inside a Peek.

## Links to other sites from Favorites and pinned tabs

A Favorite or a pinned tab keeps the page you saved, so a link that leads to a different site can open somewhere else instead of taking the tab away. Favorites open those links in a Peek unless you change it; pinned tabs follow them in the tab until you choose otherwise. Each has its own setting with the same choices: Peek, Little Arx, a new tab, or **Current Tab** to follow the link like any other. With Peek chosen, a link to `github.com` in a mail Favorite opens in a Peek, while the mail stays in the tab. Links within the same site, such as from one `google.com` page to another or to `mail.google.com`, open in the tab as usual.

Only a plain click on a link counts. Forms you submit, pages that redirect you on their own and links a page follows by itself stay in the tab. Tabs in folders count as pinned tabs; unpinned tabs, Little Arx and private windows always open links in the tab.

### New tabs a page opens

A page in a Favorite or a pinned tab can open a new tab itself: a link made to open in a new tab, a page's own button that opens one, or a form sent to a new tab. Choosing another account in Gmail's account switcher is one. That tab follows the same setting, whatever site it's on, so with Peek chosen the other account opens in a Peek over your mail. With Little Arx it opens in Little Arx; with New Tab or Current Tab it opens as a new tab, as it would in any other browser.

The page in the Peek or Little Arx is the one the page opened, so it can still talk to the page that opened it. Some sites need that: a sign-in that finishes by telling the page behind it.

These don't follow the setting:

- A link you open in a new tab yourself, with :keys{combo="⌘ Click"}, the middle button or any other click with a modifier key, opens a tab as usual.
- A small window a page opens at a size of its own, as most sign-ins with another account do, opens in Little Arx.
- A page that isn't a web page, such as an empty one a page fills in itself, opens as a new tab.

When Force Click opens a Peek, the trackpad gives a light tap, and the card grows from the point you pressed. With **Reduce motion** on in System Settings → Accessibility → Display, the card fades in instead of growing, and the page behind dims without shrinking.

## When you're done

The buttons beside the card name themselves when you hold the pointer over them.

- **Close it** with the button, :keys{combo="Esc"}, or a click outside the card.
- **Open as Tab** keeps the page in the current space, right below the tab the Peek opened over. Over a Favorite or a pinned tab, the page becomes the first unpinned tab.
- **Open in Little Arx** moves it to the [small window](/tabs/little-arx).

With [developer mode](/developers/developer-mode) on, DevTools open for a Peek too.
