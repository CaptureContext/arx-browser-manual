When you close an ordinary open tab, it goes to the Tab Archive instead of disappearing. Close freely; anything can come back.

::preview{scene="tab-archive" caption="Closed tabs wait in the Tab Archive: search for one and restore it."}
::

## What goes in

- Open tabs you close with :keys{combo="⌘ W"}.
- **Archive Selected Tab** and **Archive All Tabs in This Space**, or **Clear** above your open tabs in the sidebar.
- The page of a [Little Arx](/tabs/little-arx) window you close, in the archive of the window's space. It comes back as an ordinary tab, never on its own when Arx starts.

Private windows, pinned tabs and Favorites being reset, and tabs inside a deleted folder don't go into the archive. Entries don't expire.

## Automatic archiving

A regular tab you haven't looked at for 6 hours moves to the archive by itself, so a tab you leave alone is out of the sidebar the same day. Everything it held comes back from here. Each profile chooses how long in **Settings → Profiles → Automatically archive tabs**: **After 6 hours** (the default), **After 12 hours**, **After 1 day**, **After 7 days**, **After 30 days** or **Never**. While the archive is empty, it says how long its profile waits.

- Pinned tabs, the tabs in folders and Favorites are never archived, and neither is a tab on screen in any window, in Peek or in Little Arx, or the tab a space has selected.
- A tab playing sound or in Picture in Picture, in a call, on a site you [keep loaded](/tabs/sidebar#unloaded-tabs), or one an assistant is working in, stays too. Its clock starts again when it stops.
- A tab's clock starts when it leaves the screen. Tabs you had before choosing a shorter time start counting from then, so nothing is archived all at once.
- A tab you leave alone [unloads](/tabs/sidebar#unloaded-tabs) first, after 3 hours by default, so it stops using memory well before it's archived.
- Arx checks every few minutes. A page that asks before closing, for example with unsaved form input, may show its question. If you stay, the tab stays, and Arx doesn't ask again until the interval has passed once more.
- Automatically archived tabs come back the same way as any others.

## Bring tabs back

- :keys{combo="⇧ ⌘ T"} reopens the last closed tab.
- **Archive → Recently Closed** lists the windows you closed since Arx opened and the last 15 tabs closed in the front window's profile, with their icons. Choose a tab to restore it as :keys{combo="⇧ ⌘ T"} does, or a window to open it again.
- **File → Reopen Closed Window** opens the last window you closed, on the space and tab it showed. Spaces and tabs stay when a window closes, so nothing else needs bringing back; a Split View comes back with its tab. Private windows aren't kept.
- The **Tab Archive** (Library → Archive, or the Archive menu) lists everything, newest first, with a search. Choose **Restore**, **Restore Tabs Closed Together** for it and every tab archived with it at once, or **Restore Tabs Closed Together To** another space. Restoring into another profile asks first.

A restored tab brings back its address, its title and its back-and-forward history, not what was on the page. So do tabs after you quit and reopen Arx, and a duplicated tab starts with the history of the tab it came from. **Clear History** forgets that history too, and private windows never keep it.

## Clean up

**Delete Selected** removes entries, and **Clear This Profile's Archive** empties it. Each profile has its own archive.
