## Search engine per profile

Each [profile](/profiles/profiles) has its own search engine, set in **Settings → Profiles**: Google (the default), DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave Search, Kagi, Ecosia, Startpage or Perplexity.

## Bangs

Bangs work out of the box. Start with `!` and a keyword to search one site:

- `!yt lofi` and :keys{combo="Return"} searches YouTube for "lofi" at once.
- `!yt` alone and :keys{combo="Return"}, :keys{combo="Tab"} or :keys{combo="Space"} starts a YouTube search: the field shows YouTube and waits for what to look for. Choosing a bang from the list that `!` shows does the same.
- In that search, :keys{combo="Return"} before you type anything opens the site's front page, such as youtube.com. :keys{combo="Delete"} in the empty field, or :keys{combo="Esc"}, leaves it.

::preview{scene="bangs" caption="A bang searches one site directly; a search shortcut's keyword and Tab does the same."}
::

Arx looks for the keyword in your own search shortcuts first, then in its bundled list of over 13,000 bangs from Helium, such as `!gh` for GitHub, `!yt` for YouTube, `!w` for Wikipedia and `!mdn` for MDN. The list ships with Arx and works offline. A bang it doesn't know shows a message and suggestions; it's never quietly treated as a normal search.

Each bang in the list shows its site's icon when the profile already has it from a visit to the site's front page; otherwise it shows the magnifying glass. Typing `!` or moving through the list never makes Arx ask a site for anything. When you start a bang's search, for example `!yt` and :keys{combo="Return"}, the search field shows the site's icon; if the profile doesn't have it yet, Arx asks that site for it then and keeps it for next time, unless **Fetch icons for tabs that haven’t loaded** is off in Settings › General. A private window shows no bang icons and never asks.

## Your search shortcuts

A search shortcut is your own keyword and URL template, for a site the bundled bangs don't cover or one you want to search differently. It works exactly as a bang does, with `!` and the keyword, and also without the `!`: type the keyword and press :keys{combo="Tab"}. A bundled bang needs its `!`, because many of its keywords are ordinary words. Tab starts a site search only after a keyword; after other text it starts a [chat](/command-bar/chat). It never opens a result. Your shortcut owns its keyword, so `!yt` uses yours if you make one called `yt`, even while it's switched off.

Arx starts with none of its own, since the bangs cover the common sites. Manage yours in **Settings → General → Search shortcuts**, a page with a list like [Air Traffic Control](/profiles/air-traffic-control)'s routes:

- **Add Shortcut** opens a form. Fill in the name, keyword, search URL and an optional homepage, then press **Add** (:keys{combo="↩"}) or **Cancel** (:keys{combo="Esc"}).
- Each shortcut has a switch to turn it off, **Edit**, which opens the same form with **Save** and **Remove**, and a remove button. Right-click a shortcut for Edit and Remove Shortcut.
- The template needs exactly one `{query}`, as a whole query value or a path segment.
- Keywords must be unique and use letters, numbers, dashes or underscores.
- The bundled bangs are listed below, read-only. To find one, click the magnifier in the page's bar, or press :keys{combo="⌘ F"}, and type: while you search, only the matching bangs show.
