Each profile keeps its own addresses and payment cards. A card saved in your personal profile never shows up in your work profile, and private windows never fill either.

## Filling

Click a field of an address or checkout form, such as the name, street or card number, and Arx lists the matching addresses or cards right below it. Pick one and Arx fills the form's fields.

::preview{scene="card-fill"}
::

- Click an entry, or use the **↓** and **↑** keys, then **Return**. **Esc** closes the list.
- A card always asks for Touch ID or your Mac password before Arx fills it. The list shows only the card's network and last four digits.
- **Manage…** at the bottom of the list opens this profile's addresses and cards in Settings.
- Turn either kind off in **Settings → General → Autofill**: **Fill addresses** and **Fill payment cards** are on by default. While one is off, Settings can't add or change that kind either.

Arx doesn't offer to save an address or card you type into a page. Add them in Settings.

## Managing addresses and cards

Open **Settings → Profiles**, pick a profile, and click **Addresses and Cards** under its data.

- **Add Address…** asks for a name, street, city, postal code, country code, phone and email. Fill in what you use.
- **Add Card…** asks for the card number, the name on the card, its expiry and an optional nickname. Arx checks the number before it saves it.
- **Edit** changes an address or a card. For a card, leave the number empty to keep the saved one.
- **Remove** deletes it from this profile. Arx asks first.

Settings shows only a card's last four digits. The number stays in the profile's own folder, encrypted with Arx's key in your macOS Keychain, the same key that protects your passwords and cookies.

Importing addresses and cards from other browsers is planned.
