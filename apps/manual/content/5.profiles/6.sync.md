Arx syncs through your own iCloud account. There's no Arx account, nothing passes through Arx's servers, and sync is free. Nothing syncs until you turn it on: when you first open Arx (see below), or in **Settings → Sync** with **Sync with iCloud**. **Status** beside it says whether this Mac is syncing, and why it's paused if it is.

## What syncs

Each kind of data has its own switch under **Sync with iCloud**:

| Switch | What it syncs | Starts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Profiles and spaces** | Profiles, spaces, folders, pinned tabs and Favorites | On |
| **Preferences** | Appearance, search shortcuts, keyboard shortcuts, routing and Proxy Link rules | On |
| **History** | Pages you visited recently, in the same profile on your other Macs, from the last few days you choose in **Sync history from the last**. Clearing history on one Mac clears it on the others. | Off |
| **Tab Archive** | Tabs archived on one Mac, in the same profile's Tab Archive on your other Macs. Restoring one keeps it on the others. | Off |

Within Preferences, **Appearance**, **Search shortcuts**, **Keyboard shortcuts and aliases** and **Routing** each have a switch of their own; turned off, that part stays as this Mac has it.

Website data (cookies and sign-ins), passwords and open tabs stay on each Mac, and so do your windows and assistant grants.

Something you delete stays on your other Macs. **Sync deletions after 30 days** is a choice each Mac makes for itself: something deleted on one Mac goes from the others once it was deleted 30 days ago, none opened or edited it since, and every Mac that still has it has the option on. A Mac with it off keeps it, and so do the others meanwhile.

### Deleting a profile or space another Mac syncs

When another Mac syncs Profiles and spaces, **Delete Space** and **Remove Profile…** ask what to do with it:

- **Delete from This Mac Only**, the default: it goes from this Mac and stays on your other Macs, unmarked. It's removed for good only if every Mac deletes it.
- **Remove from iCloud**: it goes from this Mac and asks your other Macs first. Nothing goes from another Mac right away. Each of them shows a warning triangle on it, in the space switcher and in **Settings → Profiles**:
  - With **Sync deletions after 30 days** on there and on every other Mac that still has it, the hint says which Mac removed it and the day Arx removes it, unless it's opened before then.
  - Otherwise the hint says it stays there until you remove it. It keeps syncing among the Macs that keep it.

On a marked profile or space, **Remove Here Too** deletes it on that Mac now, and **Keep** clears the mark on every Mac and cancels the removal. The Mac that removed it doesn't get it back.

Folders, pinned tabs and Favorites don't ask: deleting one works like **Remove from iCloud**, without a mark. A profile kept on this Mac only, and its spaces, never ask.

## During setup

When this Mac is signed in to iCloud, the welcome asks **Your Arx on every Mac.** right after its first page. Nothing is set up yet at that point, so Arx can start from what's in iCloud instead of making a profile of its own.

- **Sync with iCloud** turns sync on and brings what your other Macs sync, one part at a time: profiles, spaces and pinned tabs, then settings, and history or Tab Archive if you sync them.
  - If iCloud already has your Arx, this Mac uses it as it is, with no extra **Default** profile, and the browser window opens on the space you used last on any Mac.
  - If iCloud has nothing yet, this Mac's **Default** profile and space go to iCloud and start it.
- If it takes more than about 20 seconds, or iCloud can't be reached, **Continue without waiting** lets you go on with **Default**, kept on this Mac until iCloud answers. If iCloud had your Arx and the welcome is still open, it replaces Default as above. Otherwise Default starts syncing too.
- **Not now** leaves sync off. You can turn it on any time in **Settings → Sync**.

If this Mac isn't signed in to iCloud, the welcome skips this page. When your setup came from iCloud, the import page says so and offers only what iCloud doesn't sync: passwords and sign-ins, history, open tabs and extensions from another browser.

## Turning sync on for a Mac you already use

If iCloud already has your workspace from another Mac, Arx merges what belongs together instead of copying everything twice. Before anything is sent, a preview shows what happens:

- **Will merge:** profiles with the same name, such as Default and Default. Within them, the space each Mac started with merges with the other's, spaces with the same name merge, and so do spaces whose pinned tabs are mostly the same. Pins and Favorites with the same address become one, and this Mac's others are added after iCloud's, in this Mac's order.
- **Will be added:** what iCloud has nothing like. If its name is taken, the Mac's name is added after it, such as "Work (MacBook Air)".
- **Settings from iCloud:** for merged profiles and spaces, iCloud's name, icon and colors win. The preview lists what changes on this Mac.

Each merge can be switched to **Keep separate**. **Merge and Sync** goes ahead; **Cancel** turns sync off again, and nothing is sent. A merge that only folds an untouched starter profile into iCloud's happens without asking. Offline, Arx waits until it can see what iCloud has.

A merged profile keeps this Mac's sign-ins, cookies and site data. Only the Mac that turns sync on merges; your other Macs see the result.

### Profiles that are already doubled

If two Macs synced before merging existed, you may have two profiles with the same name. **Settings → Sync** offers **Merge "Default" and "Default"…**, with the same preview. The first one in the profile switcher stays. Each Mac moves its open tabs to it and keeps the sign-ins of the one it used last.

iCloud's own data protection applies. End-to-end encryption depends on whether you turned on Advanced Data Protection for your Apple Account.
