Each profile has its own cookies, site data, history, extensions, Favorites and search engine. Spaces belong to a profile, so signing in to work never touches home.

::preview{scene="profiles"}
::

## Make a profile

Choose **Profiles → New profile** from the Library's menu, or **New Profile** in **Settings → Profiles**, and give it a name. Then give it a space: make a new one, or move one over with the space's **Set Profile**.

## Per-profile settings

**Settings → Profiles** lists your profiles, each with how many spaces use it. Click one to open its settings on a page of their own (**Back** or :keys{combo="⌘ ["} returns to the list). Each profile has:

- **Search engine.** See [Search and bangs](/command-bar/search-and-bangs).
- **Search suggestions**, on by default. See [Command bar](/command-bar/command-bar#search-suggestions).
- **Automatically archive tabs**, after 6 hours by default, or **Never**. See [Tab Archive](/tabs/archive#automatic-archiving).
- **Show the full URL in the toolbar**, off by default, so the address bar shows the site's name. **View → Always Show Full URL** turns it on or off for the front window's profile.
- **Appearance**: the same theme panel as a space (colors, light or dark, intensity and texture), [Match site](/start/settings#match-site) and [accent color](/start/settings#accent-color). See [Settings](/start/settings#window-background).
- **Sync with iCloud**, when iCloud Sync is on: off keeps this profile on this Mac only. **Sync Settings** under it opens [**Settings → Sync**](/profiles/sync).
- **Downloads**: the folder downloads go to, your Downloads folder at first, and **Ask where to save each file**. See [Downloads](/pages/downloads#where-files-go).
- **Browsing History**, with **Clear History**. Clearing it also forgets the words of the pages it lists, which the command bar [searches](/command-bar/command-bar), and doesn't remove saved tabs or site data.
- **Site Settings** and **Passwords**, each in a sheet of its own. See [Site settings](/profiles/site-settings) and [Passwords](/profiles/passwords).
- **Clear browsing data**: history, the downloads list, cookies, cache, autofill and site settings, by time range. See [Clear browsing data](/profiles/clear-browsing-data).
- **Extensions**. See [Extensions](/profiles/extensions).
- **Linked spaces**: the spaces that use it.

## Rename and remove

Rename or remove a profile in **Settings → Profiles**: right-click it in the list, or use **Rename…** and **Remove Profile…** at the bottom of its page. You can remove a profile once it has no spaces left, and one profile always remains.

Removing a profile keeps its website data on disk by default. To delete it too, choose **Permanently erase this profile's website data** and type the profile's name. Arx finishes erasing after you quit it, and the files are gone after the next restart of your Mac.

## Moving between profiles

Tabs and spaces can move to another profile, but their cookies and signed-in sessions stay behind: the pages reopen fresh in the new profile, and Arx asks before it does that.
