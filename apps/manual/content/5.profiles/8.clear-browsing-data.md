Each profile clears its own data. Clearing your work profile leaves your personal one as it was, and private windows keep nothing to clear.

Open **Settings → Profiles**, pick a profile, and click **Clear browsing data** under its data. To clear one site's cookies or cache, use the [site menu](/pages/site-menu#connection) instead.

::preview{scene="clear-browsing-data" caption="Clear browsing data for a profile: switch the range to All time and the counts update, check Download history, uncheck Cached images and files, then Clear Data."}
::

## Time range

Pick how far back to go: **Last hour** (the default), **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 4 weeks** or **All time**. Older data stays.

## What you can clear

Each row shows how much that range holds, such as the number of pages or sites, before you clear anything.

| Row | What goes | Checked at first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browsing history | Pages you visited, as Arx lists them in History and command bar suggestions, and the pictures of pages [hover previews](/tabs/sidebar#tabs-that-arent-loaded) saved in that range | Yes |
| Download history | Finished downloads in the [Downloads](/pages/downloads) list. The files stay where they are, and downloads still running stay too. | No |
| Cookies and other site data | Cookies and everything else sites store. This signs you out of most sites. | Yes |
| Cached images and files | Copies of pages and images kept to load sites faster. For a time range, Arx shows the most it could be. | Yes |
| Autofill form data | Form entries, addresses and payment cards saved for autofill | No |
| Site settings | Permissions and page zoom you set for sites. See [Site settings](/profiles/site-settings). | No |

**Clear Data**, at the bottom of the sheet, removes the checked rows and closes it. If something couldn't be cleared completely, the sheet stays open and names it. To leave without clearing anything, click **Close** (×) at the top or press :keys{combo="esc"}.

Saved passwords aren't cleared here. Manage them in [Passwords](/profiles/passwords).

If your organization keeps browsing history, Arx says so and clears nothing.
