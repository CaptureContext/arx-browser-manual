---
title: Find in page
icon: raycast:magnifying-glass
description: Find text on the page you're reading with ⌘F, and step through the matches.
status: ready
---

Press :keys{combo="⌘ F"}. A small bar opens at the top right of the page with the number of matches.

If the bar is already open and you've clicked into the page, the address bar or the sidebar, :keys{combo="⌘ F"} puts you back in the bar with its text selected, so you can type a new search straight away. It works the same in reading mode.

::shortcut-table
---
rows:
  - [Find in Page, ⌘ F]
  - [Next match, ⌘ G]
  - [Previous match, ⇧ ⌘ G]
---
::

The bar belongs to the page: switching tabs closes it.
