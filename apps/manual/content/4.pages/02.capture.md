Choose **File → Capture Area…** or **Capture Visible Area…**, or find them in the command bar or the [site menu](/pages/site-menu). Neither has a default key; you can add one in [Settings → Shortcuts](/start/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Capture part of the page

**Capture Area…** works on the page itself. The whole page dims, a pill at the bottom says **Click or drag to capture a portion of this page**, and the pointer turns into a crosshair with a small camera badge. The crosshair's center is the point you pick with.

- **Pick a block.** Move the pointer over the page: the block under it, such as a paragraph, an image or a section, gets an outline. Click to capture it.
- **Draw an area.** Drag across the page to capture exactly what you cover.
- **Use the keyboard.** ← and → move the outline to the previous or next block, ↑ to the block around it and ↓ to the first block inside it. Return captures the outlined block.

Whatever you're about to capture stays clear while the rest of the page stays dim, and the clear area glides from block to block as you move the pointer or drag. With **Reduce motion** on in System Settings → Accessibility → Display, it moves without animating.

You can scroll while you pick. Press **Esc** at any point to stop.

In a private window, and on pages that aren't ordinary web pages, Arx can't see the page's blocks, so drag to draw an area instead.

## Capture what's on screen

**Capture Visible Area…** captures the visible part of the page straight away.

## After capturing

The capture stays framed on the page, with a menu beside it:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Copy** (⌘C) | Puts the image on the clipboard. |
| **Save…** | Saves a PNG where you choose. |
| **Edit** | Opens the image in Preview, where you can mark it up. |
| **Send to Chat** | Attaches the image to the window's [chat](/command-bar/chat#give-it-context) and opens the chat panel if it's hidden. Not in private windows or Little Arx. |
| **Retake** | Clears the frame so you can pick or draw again. |
| **Capture Full Page** | After Capture Visible Area…, captures the whole page, top to bottom, instead. |

Copy, Save…, Edit and Send to Chat finish the capture. Press **Esc** or click away from the frame to finish without keeping it. Switching tabs finishes it too.

With [Sound feedback](/start/settings#sound-feedback) on, Copy and Save… play a short sound once the image is on the clipboard or in its file, and a capture that can't be taken, copied or saved plays a low one.

Captures keep every pixel of your display, so on a Retina display they're twice the size in pixels of what you selected in points.

If the capture covers a video that came out blank, Arx says so under the menu. Some sites protect their videos so they can't be captured.
