Zoom a page in or out from the **View** menu or the keyboard. Arx steps through the same levels as Chrome, from 25% to 500%.

::preview{scene="zoom" caption="⌘+ zooms the whole site and shows its level for a moment; ⌘− steps back and ⌘0 resets."}
::

::shortcut-table
---
rows:
  - [Zoom In, ⌘ =]
  - [Zoom Out, ⌘ -]
  - [Actual Size, ⌘ 0]
---
::

:keys{combo="⌘ +"} zooms in too. Each zoom shows the new level for a moment at the top of the page. The commands are also in the [Command Bar](/command-bar/command-bar), and you can change their keys in **Settings → Shortcuts**.

## Zoom belongs to the site

A zoom applies to the whole site, as in Chrome: zoom in on one GitHub page and every GitHub page in that profile follows, in every tab and after you quit. The site shows up under **Page Zoom** in the profile's [Site settings](/profiles/site-settings), where you can change or remove it. **Actual Size** goes back to the profile's **Default zoom**.

In a private window, a zoom lasts only until you close that window. A page without a site address, such as a local file, keeps its zoom to its own tab until you leave it.
