Reading mode shows a page's article without the page around it: its title, byline, words and pictures, set in a column in one of four themes. It's laid over the page rather than loaded in its place, so closing it shows the page exactly as you left it, scrolled where it was, with nothing reloaded and Back and Forward unchanged.

Arx is testing reading mode. It's on in the beta.

## Open it

When a page looks like it has an article, such as a news story or a Wikipedia page, a page button shows in the address bar, next to the link button. Then any of these opens reading mode:

- The page button in the address bar.
- :keys{combo="⌃ ⌘ R"}, or **View → Reading Mode**.
- **Reading Mode** in the [command bar](/command-bar/command-bar).
- **Reading Mode** at the top of the [site menu](/pages/site-menu).

The page button fills in while reading mode is open. A page with no article says so instead.

Arx checks a page for an article once it has finished loading, so the page button comes a moment after the page does. When you go from an article to another page, the button's place in the address bar stays empty, not taken by the address, until Arx has checked the new page: the address moves once, if at all, instead of twice.

## Read

The controls float at the top right:

- **Light**, **Sepia**, **Gray** and **Dark** themes.
- **Smaller Text** and **Larger Text**.
- **Narrower Column** and **Wider Column**.
- **Exit Reading Mode**.

Arx remembers your theme, size and width for every article. Scroll with the trackpad, the arrow keys or the space bar. A link to a place in the article, such as a footnote, scrolls there; any other link closes reading mode and opens in the page.

## Close it

Press :keys{combo="esc"}, :keys{combo="⌃ ⌘ R"} again, the page button, or **Exit Reading Mode**. Going to another page closes it too, and so does reloading the page, whether you reload it, the page reloads itself or a script does, and [Find](/pages/find-in-page), which searches the page itself. After a reload, Arx checks the page for an article again. Moving to a place within the page, or a part of it loading, such as an ad, leaves reading mode open.

## Split View, Peek and Little Arx

Reading mode belongs to one page. In [Split View](/tabs/split-view), each pane's header has its own page button, and the shortcut acts on the focused pane. It works the same in Peek and Little Arx.

## What it can't read

Private windows, [WebKit pages](/pages/protected-video), PDFs and pages that aren't on the web don't have reading mode. Reading mode finds the article with Mozilla's Readability, on your Mac: nothing is sent anywhere, and the page itself isn't changed.

To turn it off, turn off its feature flag in **Settings → Advanced → Feature Flags** and relaunch Arx, or quit Arx and run:

```bash
defaults write dev.capturecontext.arx ARX_READING_MODE -string off
```

To turn it back on, turn the flag back on in the same place, or quit Arx and run:

```bash
defaults delete dev.capturecontext.arx ARX_READING_MODE
```
