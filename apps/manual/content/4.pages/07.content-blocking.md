Arx blocks ads, trackers and known malware sites with [uBlock Origin](https://github.com/gorhill/uBlock), which is built into Arx. It's on in every profile and in private windows. You don't need to install anything.

::

## Turn it on or off

**Settings → General → Content blocking** has:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Block ads and trackers | On | uBlock Origin filters every page, in every profile and in private windows. Off stops it everywhere. |
| Block cookie banners | Off | Hides cookie consent pop-ups with EasyList's and uBlock Origin's cookie lists. If a site stops working, turn blocking off for it. |
| Filter lists and advanced settings | | **Open uBlock Origin** opens uBlock Origin's own dashboard in a new tab, in the profile of the front window. |

## Turn it off for one site

If a site doesn't work with blocking on, open the [site menu](/pages/site-menu) and switch **Block ads and trackers** off. The menu shows **Off for this site**. Reload the page to see it without blocking. Switch it back on the same way.

It's per profile: the site is still blocked in your other profiles. In uBlock Origin's dashboard the site appears under **Trusted sites**.

## Hide an element on a site

Something on a site you'd rather not see, such as a banner, a sidebar or a box of suggestions, can stay hidden for good. Press :keys{combo="⇧ ⌘ H"}, or choose **View → Hide Element…**, the **Hide Element…** button in the [site menu](/pages/site-menu) or **Hide Element…** in the command bar. It works the same in a Split View pane, in Peek and in [Little Arx](/tabs/little-arx).

The pointer turns into a scope, and whatever is under it gets a red outline with its name above it. Click to hide it: it fades away, and stays hidden on that site after you reload or restart Arx. You stay in Hide Element, so you can go on clicking other things. :keys{combo="↑"} widens the outline to the element around it and :keys{combo="↓"} narrows it back; :keys{combo="Space"} hides what's outlined.

A toast at the bottom right of the page says what a click does, with **Undo** (:keys{combo="⌘ Z"}), which brings back the last thing you hid, and **Done** (:keys{combo="esc"}). It moves to the bottom left, or fades, when what you're outlining is under it, and stays put and solid once the pointer is on it, so a click on it is always the toast's.

Press :keys{combo="↩"}, or click the arrow in the toast, to open the list of everything hidden on the site: each entry with its name, what kind of element it is and its CSS selector. Click the eye beside one to show it again, or choose it with :keys{combo="↑"} and :keys{combo="↓"} and press :keys{combo="⌫"}. Something you hid earlier comes back when you click **Done**, which reloads the page. :keys{combo="↩"} or :keys{combo="esc"} closes the list.

To bring one back later, open the site menu. **Hidden Elements** lists the same entries. Click **Show** beside one and the page reloads with it back.

Hidden elements are rules in uBlock Origin's **My filters**, kept with the profile, so other profiles still show them. Hide Element works on web pages while content blocking is on, and not in private windows. Rules you add yourself in the dashboard's **My filters** that hide elements on one named site (`example.com##.banner`) show in the site menu too; rules for several sites stay in the dashboard.

## Filter lists

uBlock Origin starts with its default lists: its own filters, EasyList, EasyPrivacy, a malware list and Peter Lowe's list. It updates them on its own from the people who publish them, such as the uBlock Origin project, EasyList and AdGuard, as it does in other browsers. Those servers see your Mac's IP address when a list updates, and nothing about the pages you visit.

To add lists, such as one for your language or for annoyances like newsletter pop-ups, open the dashboard (**Open uBlock Origin**) and go to **Filter lists**. Changes there are uBlock Origin's own and apply to that profile.

## Private windows

Private windows are blocked too, the way the profile you opened the window from is: with its lists, the sites you turned blocking off for in it, and your **Block cookie banners** setting.
