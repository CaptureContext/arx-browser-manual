Some sites talk to devices plugged into your Mac or nearby: firmware flashers, security key setup pages, 3D printers, Arduino and microcontroller tools. They use WebUSB, WebHID, Web Serial or Web Bluetooth.

## Choosing a device

When a site asks for a device, Arx shows a sheet on its window, titled with the site and what it asks for, and lists the devices that match. Pick one and click **Connect**, or double-click it. **Cancel** or :keys{combo="Esc"} tells the site no device was chosen.

- The list updates as devices are plugged in or come into range.
- For Bluetooth, **Scan Again** looks for nearby devices again, and each device shows its signal and whether it's paired or connected. If Bluetooth is off, the sheet says so.
- A site asks only after you click or press a key on it. Arx shows one request at a time on a page.

The first time a site uses Bluetooth, macOS may ask whether Arx may use Bluetooth.

## Devices a site may use

Once you connect a device, the site may use it again without asking. To take that back:

- open the [site menu](/pages/site-menu): the **Devices** rows list what this site may use, and **Forget** removes one, or
- open **Settings → Profiles →** a profile **→ Site Settings**: **Devices** lists every site's devices in that profile.

Each profile keeps its own. Private windows remember nothing after they close.

::note
Headless runs (`Arx --headless`) cancel every device request at once, since nobody can choose a device.
::

WebKit pages don't show device requests: see [Netflix, Spotify and protected video](/pages/protected-video).
