Arx translates pages on your Mac with Apple's Translation framework, the same as Safari. Nothing you read leaves the Mac.

## Translate Page

Choose **View → Translate Page**, find **Translate Page** in the [command bar](/command-bar/command-bar), or open the [site menu](/pages/site-menu): when the page is in another language than yours, it offers **Translate from: French** (or the page's language). Arx replaces the page's text with its translation, into your Mac's first preferred language.

- Text that scrolls into view, or that the page adds later, is translated as it appears.
- **Show Original** (the same command, or the site menu's switch) brings the page's own text back.
- Going to another page ends the translation.

Code, form fields, text you're editing and parts the site marks as not to translate stay as they are.

## Languages

macOS translates only between languages it has downloaded. If a language isn't there yet, Arx says so: download it in **System Settings → General → Language & Region → Translation Languages**, then translate again.

## Never and Always

In the site menu of a translated page:

- **Never translate this site** stops Arx from translating the site on its own, and from offering it in the site menu.
- **Always translate French** (the page's language) translates every page in that language as soon as it loads, in this profile.

Each profile remembers its own choices.

## What isn't translated

Private windows and [WebKit pages](/pages/protected-video) aren't translated, and neither are PDFs or pages that aren't on the web.

## Translation Available

Arx is testing a new way to translate, after Arc's. It's on in the beta:

- When a page is in another language, the address bar's second line says **Translation Available**. Click it: **Translate this page to English?** offers **Translate**, and **Options** holds **Choose Another Language**, **Never Translate This Site** and **Language Settings**. Once the page is translated, the same line opens **Show Original**.
- If macOS hasn't downloaded the page's language yet, **Download** asks for it right there.
- Each sentence is translated whole, with its links, so a link stays on the words it became.
- Private windows translate too. They follow the choices of the profile that opened them, and forget their own when they close.
- **Settings → Profiles →** a profile **→ Translation choices → Reset** forgets that profile's **Never translate** sites and **Always translate** languages, so Arx asks again. It's dimmed while there's nothing to forget.
- **Settings → General → Translation** chooses who translates: **Apple, on this Mac** (the default; the page's text doesn't leave your Mac) or **Google**, which sends the text of each page you translate to Google. With Apple, **Quality** is **Auto** (what macOS prefers), **Apple Intelligence quality** or **Faster**. **Translate into** picks the language, your preferred one by default.

To turn it off, turn off its feature flag in **Settings → Advanced → Feature Flags** and relaunch Arx, or quit Arx and run:

```bash
defaults write dev.capturecontext.arx ARX_TRANSLATE -string off
```

To turn it back on, turn the flag back on in the same place, or quit Arx and run:

```bash
defaults delete dev.capturecontext.arx ARX_TRANSLATE
```
