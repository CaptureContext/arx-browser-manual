**Help → Send Feedback…** (**⌘⌥⇧I**, or **Send Feedback** in the command bar) opens a card over the window you're in: the browser window, or the Settings window when you ask from there. What you send there goes to Arx's maintainer. Your email, files and diagnostics are never published; the text of a report may be, as [described below](#where-it-goes) and in the privacy policy.

To report something in public instead, use **Report in public on GitHub instead** at the top of the card, which opens GitHub's issue forms for Arx.

## Writing a report

1. Pick what it is in the row under the title: **Bug Report**, **Feature Request**, **Vulnerability** or **Other**. The text field's hint changes with it, and pointing at **Vulnerability** says how such reports are handled.
2. Write what happened and what you expected, or what you'd like.
3. Add files if they help (see below).
4. Add your email in the **Email** field if you'd like a reply. Arx remembers it for next time.
5. Choose whether to **Include diagnostics**.
6. Click **Send**.

When the report has gone, the card closes. When it can't go, the card stays with your report and says why, such as no connection or too many reports in a short time. **Cancel** or **Esc** closes the card and keeps what you wrote, files included, for the next time you open it.

A **Vulnerability** report is marked urgent and stays private until the problem is fixed. You don't need a separate address for security reports.

## Files

You can attach up to 5 files, 4 MB together: pictures, videos, plain text or PDF files. There are three ways to add one:

- The paperclip in the text field's corner.
- Dropping files on the card.
- **⌘V**, which also pastes a screenshot straight from the clipboard.

Each file shows as a tile under the text; the **×** on a tile removes it. A large picture is made smaller to fit. A video, or a file that still doesn't fit, is refused with how much the files would come to; leave one out.

## Diagnostics

With **Include diagnostics** on, which it is unless you turn it off, the report also carries what [Report a Problem](/help/crash-reports#report-a-problem) saves: your Mac's model and memory, and the crash reports, freezes and unexpected quits from the last 7 days. It never carries page content, web addresses or your history. Under the switch, the card says that by sending the report you agree to Arx's [privacy policy](https://arxbrowser.net/privacy-policy); the policy's name there opens it.

The footnote at the bottom of the card names the versions that go with every report: Arx's and macOS's.

## Where it goes

Arx sends the report to its own server, which passes it to the maintainer's private issue tracker and keeps nothing of it. Your email, files and diagnostics stay private there; the maintainer may later publish what you wrote, without them, as a public GitHub issue.

Arx sends no report unless you click **Send**.
