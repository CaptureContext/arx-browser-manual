Every version of Arx, newest first, with everything that changed, is on the [Arx changelog](https://arxbrowser.net/changelog) at arxbrowser.net. The changelog has an [Atom feed](https://arxbrowser.net/changelog.xml) for your feed reader.

The **What's New** sheet that opens after an update shows the short version and links here. See [Updates](/start/updates) for how Arx updates itself and the Stable and Beta channels.

The release notes of each version are also on its [release on GitHub](https://github.com/capturecontext/arx-browser/releases), where its disk image is.

November 23, 2026. The first public release of Arx. [Read the release notes](https://arxbrowser.net/changelog).
