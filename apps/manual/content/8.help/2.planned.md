These are things Arx doesn't do yet. Each one is a plan, not a promise of a date, and it may change. The [changelog](https://arxbrowser.net/changelog) lists what's coming soon, too.

## Coming soon

- **Stability and performance**: fixes and speed-ups from what you report during the beta. See [Send feedback](/help/send-feedback).
- **Private destinations**: sending a link to a private window right from the Runway. See [Air Traffic Control](/profiles/air-traffic-control).
- **More app icons**, and polish across the app. See [Settings](/start/settings).
- **A Raycast extension** with Search Tabs, Open in Space and New Little Arx. AppleScript and `arx://` links work today. See [Shortcuts, AppleScript and arx:// links](/developers/automation).
- **Live folders** that fill themselves with your open GitHub pull requests or the ones waiting for your review, and badge what's new. Calendar meetings, documents and shared folders may follow.
- **AI commands and Assistant Features**: organizing today's tabs, tidying titles and filenames, link summaries on hover, opening the top search result, and assistant search shortcuts. See [Chat with the assistant](/command-bar/chat#settings).

## Waiting on Apple

- **Website passkeys** and **iCloud Passwords.** Apple has to approve Arx for passkey support and add it to its list of browsers. See [Extensions](/profiles/extensions).
- **Assistant sign-in with Apple Passwords, another password manager or passkeys**, without the assistant seeing the values. Saved Arx passwords already work. See [Sign-ins stay with you](/assistants/sign-ins).

## Before 1.0

- **A redesigned Little Arx**, which opens on top of full-screen apps. See [Little Arx](/tabs/little-arx).
- **Open tabs from Chrome and Dia** when you bring them in. Their bookmarks, history, passwords and sign-ins already come in. See [Bring in from another browser](/start/bring-in).
- **Importing addresses and payment cards** from other browsers, and offering to save ones you type into a page. See [Addresses and cards](/profiles/addresses-and-cards).
- **Tabs from other devices** in iCloud sync. Profiles, spaces, preferences, history and Tab Archive sync today. See [Sync](/profiles/sync).
- **Floating part of a page** in Page in Picture in Picture, such as just the video player. Floating a whole page works today. See [Media](/pages/media#page-in-picture-in-picture).
- **More hover previews**: Outlook and Notion calendars in Live Calendar, and Outlook mail and unread Gmail on Favorites. Gmail, Google Calendar and recent Linear, Notion and Figma pages work today. See [The sidebar](/tabs/sidebar#hover-previews).
- **Chat Projects**. Chat with Claude or ChatGPT works today. See [Chat with the assistant](/command-bar/chat).
