## Where do I get Arx?

[Download it](https://arxbrowser.net/download) from arxbrowser.net, or from the [latest release on GitHub](https://github.com/capturecontext/arx-browser/releases/latest). Arx is in public beta: it's ready for every day and still has rough edges. See [Getting started](/start/getting-started) for installing it, and the [release notes](/help/release-notes) for what's in each version. Once it's installed, Arx [updates itself](/start/updates).

## Is Arx open source?

Yes. The [source code is on GitHub](https://github.com/capturecontext/arx-browser), so anyone can read it, build it and check what the browser sends.

Arx is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3, like the Helium engine it's built on. Report bugs and suggest features in its [issues](https://github.com/capturecontext/arx-browser/issues), or from Arx with [Send Feedback](/help/send-feedback), and talk ideas through in its [Discussions](https://github.com/capturecontext/arx-browser/discussions). It doesn't take pull requests yet. To see the licences of everything Arx includes, choose **Arx → About Arx** and scroll the credits.

## How is Arx related to Arc?

Arx is an independent CaptureContext project inspired by Arc's approach to browsing. It is not affiliated with Arc or The Browser Company. You can [import your Arc profiles, spaces and pinned tabs](/start/bring-in) when you switch.

## Why Chromium?

For web compatibility and Chrome extensions. Arx runs a Helium-derived Chromium engine under a native macOS interface written in Swift.

## Do I need to use AI to browse?

No. Agent access is off until you connect an assistant, and each assistant only gets the spaces and capabilities you grant it. You can revoke a grant at any time.

## Can an assistant see my passwords?

No. When a site needs you to sign in, the assistant hands the page to you and every assistant pauses while you type. Arx tells the assistant whether you finished, never what you typed. If you've saved a password in Arx, an assistant can ask Arx to [sign in with it](/assistants/sign-ins#signing-in-with-a-saved-password): Arx fills it in itself and tells the assistant only whether it worked.

## Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later. Arx doesn't run on Intel Macs.

## Can Arx save my passwords?

Yes. When you sign in to a site, Arx offers to [save the password](/profiles/passwords) in that profile. You can see, add, edit, remove and import them from Chrome or Apple Passwords in [Settings](/profiles/passwords#managing-saved-passwords), one profile at a time. Website passkeys and Apple's iCloud Passwords both wait on Apple's approval.

## Does Arx work with VoiceOver and other accessibility settings?

Yes. In the sidebar, VoiceOver reads each tab or folder's name, then whether it's selected or expanded and how deep it sits in nested folders. The VoiceOver actions menu (VO-Command-Space) on a row holds what the pointer would show on hover, such as closing or resetting the tab, **Rename** and **Move Items…**. In the command bar, VoiceOver reads each result as you move through them with the arrow keys, and the URL bar reads the address it shows.

With **Reduce motion** on in System Settings → Accessibility → Display, Arx's chrome fades or switches instead of sliding, growing or drawing in. With **Increase contrast** on, dimmed text and icons in the sidebar, the URL bar and the command bar get darker in light appearance and brighter in dark appearance.

## Does Arx sync between my Macs?

Yes, through your own iCloud account, with no Arx account and nothing passing through Arx's servers. Turn it on when you first open Arx, or later in **Settings → Sync**. See [Sync](/profiles/sync). To move your setup without iCloud, use an [Arx archive](/start/export-import).

## Does Arx cost anything?

No. Arx is free, with no credits-based AI plan.
